cmix-obias integration
======================

This tree includes source changes and the BitLSTM32 FP16 model asset from:

  cmix-obias by dfreelan
  https://huggingface.co/dfreelan/cmix-obias

cmix-obias is derived from cmix-lex and is distributed under the GNU General
Public License, version 3. The corresponding GPLv3 text is included as LICENSE.

The imported material includes the coded-bit BitLSTM32 terminal correction
head, output-bias PPMd prior, flat/aligned LSTM and mixer implementation,
selected CPU/locality improvements, build parameters, documentation, and:

  models/bitlstm32/refit_golden256_fp16.blob
  SHA-256: 35cd24fed87c3409994abf5573b5697be19ea03b5ece0928b69b1cdc4f3b6078

Existing FXCM v26 and other third-party attributions in their source headers
remain unchanged.

fx2-cmix CPU transformer
========================

This tree includes the CPU-only 6M transformer inference implementation and
the published quantized model asset supplied with the fx2-cmix transformer
research bundle:

  src/third_party/fx2_transformer/
  models/transformer6m/6m-q4-fp32.tfwc2

The transformer replaces the online byte LSTM only when TRANSFORMER=1;
PPMd, FXCM, the final mixers, and the BitLSTM32 terminal head remain active.
The imported source is distributed under the GNU General Public License,
version 3. Its original license is preserved as
src/third_party/fx2_transformer/LICENSE.
